The story behind Fite Labs

Family Is The Engine and ownership is the mandate.

Fite Labs started when founder Gabriel Olinger realized that creative control and personal ownership matter. While onboarding at a prior employer, he saw contract language that would claim rights to code created inside or outside of work. That was the spark to create an independent lab focused on building useful systems while keeping ownership with the maker.

Practical wins

Real work, not hype. Examples include infrastructure automation with Python and Terraform and embedding API driven steps into deployment pipelines to remove manual tickets like IP allocation. The point is time back and fewer headaches.

Ethics that stay rooted

We avoid replacing people when a process can be enhanced instead. Many small businesses are unsure how to approach AI. We help them use it in ways that protect their teams and serve the community.

Family Is The Engine

Every build keeps families in mind, whether that is a team, a neighborhood, or the clients they support. When people understand the systems they run, they keep value close and confidence high.
